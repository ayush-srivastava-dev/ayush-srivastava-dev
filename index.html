Our Craft

Every stitch is a decision, not a default.

Nidhi's Creations began at a single cutting table, with the belief that a bag should be judged by what you can't see as much as what you can — the reinforced corners, the waxed thread, the hardware that's actually brass rather than plated.

Each piece passes through the hands of one artisan from cut to finish, so no two bags carry quite the same grain.
